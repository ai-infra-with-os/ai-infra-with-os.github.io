ACM SIGOPS ATC 2026 Tutorial Hong Kong

AI Infrastructure with Operating Systems

A half-day tutorial on the systems software beneath modern AI — from accelerator runtimes and memory hierarchies to cluster-level scheduling and resilience.

When ATC 2026 tutorial day · 14:00–18:00 + banquet
Where Hong Kong
Format In person
01

Overview

Training and serving large AI models has become a defining workload for high-end computing. The performance, efficiency and reliability of these workloads depend heavily on the operating system and the surrounding infrastructure — the runtimes, schedulers, memory managers and communication stacks that sit between hardware and model.

This tutorial surveys the design of operating systems and systems infrastructure for AI workloads. Over a single afternoon the speakers will move from foundational mechanisms to deployed systems, covering what has changed in the last few years and which problems remain open. The session is aimed at graduate students, systems researchers and engineers who want a grounded map of the area.

The tutorial is co-located with ACM SIGOPS ATC 2026 in Hong Kong and closes with a banquet, giving attendees time to talk with the speakers informally.

02

Topics

The tutorial sits at the intersection of operating systems and AI infrastructure. Indicative topics include, but are not limited to:

  • OS support for GPUs, NPUs and accelerators
  • Memory and storage hierarchy for large models
  • Scheduling for ML training and serving
  • Kernels, drivers and runtime systems for AI
  • Resource disaggregation (CXL, PIM, RDMA)
  • System observability for AI workloads
  • Large-scale model serving infrastructure
  • Checkpointing, fault tolerance and recovery
  • Collective communication optimization
  • Energy, power and thermal management
  • Confidential and trusted AI execution
  • AI for systems and systems for AI
