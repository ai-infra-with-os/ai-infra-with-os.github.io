Overview

Large-scale artificial intelligence — from foundation-model training to trillion-parameter inference and agentic systems — is reshaping the demands placed on every layer of the computing stack. Yet the systems software that mediates between models and hardware remains the linchpin of performance, efficiency, cost and reliability.

This ACM SIGOPS ATC 2026 tutorial brings together researchers and practitioners to survey the design of operating systems and infrastructure for AI workloads. Across a single afternoon we will move from first principles to deployed case studies, covering the OS, runtime and cluster-level mechanisms that make modern AI systems practical at scale.

The tutorial is co-located with ACM SIGOPS ATC 2026 in Hong Kong and concludes with a banquet overlooking Victoria Harbour — a chance to continue the conversation with speakers and fellow attendees.

Topics

The tutorial spans the intersection of operating systems and AI infrastructure. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • OS support for GPUs, NPUs & accelerators
  • Memory & storage hierarchy for large models
  • Scheduling for ML training & serving
  • Kernels, drivers & runtime systems for AI
  • Resource disaggregation (CXL, PIM, RDMA)
  • System observability for AI workloads
  • Large-scale model serving infrastructure
  • Checkpointing, fault tolerance & recovery
  • Communication & collective optimization
  • Energy, power & thermal management
  • Confidential & trusted AI execution
  • AI for systems & systems for AI
